Quantitative Formulation

Dealer gamma is derived by weighting Black-Scholes gamma $\Gamma$ by strike open interest across near-term expirations:

Γ(S) = ∑ Call_OI × Γ_C(S) × S × 100 - ∑ Put_OI × Γ_P(S) × S × 100

The Zero-Gamma Flip Level $S^*$ solves Γ(S^*) = 0. When Spot > Flip, dealers are long gamma, trading counter-trend to compress volatility. When Spot < Flip, dealers are short gamma, forced to sell declining assets, triggering violent directional continuations.
